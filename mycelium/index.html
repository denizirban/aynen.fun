Explore a living forest

Grow a fungal colony, recycle organic matter, and form symbioses with trees. Different species occupy different ecological niches. This is a game-inspired ecological model, not a scientific predictor.

  • Grow: click near existing branches to extend your network. Moist soil reduces the energy cost.
  • Absorb: click glowing amber nutrient pockets once your network is nearby.
  • Partner: connect a nearby tree to exchange resources.
  • Fruit: select a species in the Field Guide, then place its fruiting body.
  • Rainwater / Compost: manage moisture and soil health; seasons gradually change conditions.
  • Micro World: inspect underground hyphae and roots. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
  • Save: progress is saved automatically; use Export Save for a portable backup.
